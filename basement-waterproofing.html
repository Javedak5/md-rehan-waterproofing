Problems solved

What this fixes.

  • Wall and floor seepage
  • Water ingress
  • Dampness
  • Leakage

Procedure

How we do it.

1

Todai (chipping) of the floor and affected walls

2

Wall todai up to about 6 ft, where specified

3

Injection grouting at leakage points

4

Fix weld wire mesh on walls and floor

5

High-pressure guniting

6

Dewatering to lower the water level, if required

7

Leakage inspection after completion
