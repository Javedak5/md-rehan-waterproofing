Service overview
Common requirements

Where this work helps.

  • Terrace surfaces requiring slope correction
  • Water stagnation around low spots
  • Ageing or damaged terrace coba
  • Terrace surfaces requiring a protective treatment layer

Work process

How the work is carried out.

Exact steps and materials are selected after checking the site, existing surface and project requirements.

1

Inspect the terrace, existing surface, outlets and drainage levels

2

Remove loose or unsound material and prepare the base

3

Repair cracks and prepare joints and drain junctions

4

Apply the specified bonding or waterproofing treatment, where required

5

Lay brick bats in cement mortar and form the required slope towards outlets

6

Finish the surface and complete curing as specified

7

Check drainage and carry out a water test where specified

Materials

Materials used.

Brick bats
Cement
Sand
Waterproofing compound or bonding agent, where specified
Crack and joint repair materials
Protective surface finish, where specified
