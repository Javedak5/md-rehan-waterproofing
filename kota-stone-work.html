Service overview
Common requirements

Where this work helps.

  • New Kota stone flooring
  • Replacement of damaged or loose stone pieces
  • Uneven flooring and worn surfaces
  • Open or damaged flooring joints
  • Existing Kota stone floors requiring grinding or polishing

Work process

How the work is carried out.

Exact steps and materials are selected after checking the site, existing surface and project requirements.

1

Inspect the site, floor levels, base condition and required finish

2

Prepare and level the base or mortar bed

3

Cut and lay Kota stone pieces to the required layout and alignment

4

Check levels, edges, joints and transitions

5

Fill joints and clean the laid surface

6

Allow the floor to cure as required

7

Carry out grinding, polishing or sealing where specified

Materials

Materials used.

Kota stone slabs or tiles
Cement mortar
Sand
Stone joint-filling material
Grinding and polishing abrasives
Stone sealer, where specified
